    The software that creates TigerTag+ Certified spools

    Everything a production line needs, in one app.

    Fast Burning — 1 second per spool

    Auto mode: the operator places the spool, the chips are written, signed and verified. Up to 1,200 spools an hour per station.

    Fast Check — quality control in 0.2 s

    Check any spool against the expected product: data, origin signature, twin tags. A non-conforming spool triggers a full-screen alert.

    Certified, impossible to counterfeit

    Every chip carries 100 % of its critical data and an unforgeable proof of origin, verifiable offline on any phone.

    Scan to load

    The operator scans the spool's barcode or types its SKU: no long list, no mis-click.

    Error-proof

    Your product file is checked on load and incomplete products are blocked before they reach a chip. Optional batch numbers, production logs per brand.

    Built for the factory floor

    Works fully offline, updates itself, speaks 11 languages, runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

    From licence to production in four steps

    1. Get your licence

      TigerTag issues a TigerTag+ licence for your brand.

    2. Install and add the licence

      Download the app above and drop the licence file on it.

    3. Load your catalogue

      Export your materials from TigerTag Manager and drop the file into the app.

    4. Burn

      Place the spool on the reader and scan its barcode — the chips are written in about a second.

    Try before you commit

    Ask for a Free Sample licence: test TigerTag+ on your line with a daily limit of spools; sample chips are marked « Free Sample Not For Sell ».

    How the tag gets into your spools

    A carrier, not a retooling. It holds two independent NTAG chips, one at each folded end, so the spool reads from either side; 3M adhesive holds it. Your operator peels and sticks — nothing else on the line changes.
